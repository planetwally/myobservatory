Bar height is the amount: more cloud, more moonlight, closer to dew, stronger gusts. Seeing, transparency, target altitude and the Go row grow taller as they improve. Colour is always the verdict. ▼ marks the target's meridian transit. Tap any hour for the numbers.

Forecast: Open-Meteo; target names resolved by CDS Sesame. Seeing and transparency are heuristics from jet-stream winds, humidity and aerosols, the same ones the Sky Planner uses. The dew row assumes optics run up to ~2.5 °C (4.5 °F) below air temperature under a clear, still sky. Treat all of it as guidance, not gospel.
